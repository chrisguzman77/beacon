# firmware

Owners: Slavik (pod/, controlbox/, all wiring) and Chris (gateway/, taken over 2026-10-17 so Slavik can build the device; he had not started it). Three programs.
- pod/ MicroPython on the Waveshare RP2040-Zero. Reads the LD2410B radar on UART0 (GP0/GP1), the photoresistor at 5 kHz on core 1 for lightning flashes, the water sensor, the DHT11, the tilt switch, and the IR receiver. Streams one line every 100 ms plus immediate FLASH and IR lines. See contracts/serial.md.
- controlbox/ Arduino sketch on the UNO R3. Drives the 4 digit countdown through the 74HC595, the WS2812B LED strip status light on D6, the servo gauge needle, and the LCD1602 on the beacon head as the beacon's text display. Parses commands from the Pi. See contracts/serial.md.
- gateway/ Python on the Raspberry Pi. Opens both serial ports, runs the local rules (flash to bang distance, heat, water, tilt), holds the equipment interlock on GPIO 18 and the e-stop on GPIO 23, handles the IR remote (crew check in by digit, supervisor PIN with OK digits OK, briefing UP English and DOWN Spanish), plays voice clips, connects to the backend websocket (contracts/gateway-ws.md), and queues events while offline.

Fixed: the pin maps and protocols in docs/hardware-guide.md and contracts/serial.md. The interlock fails safe (GPIO 18 low stops the motor). The Pi is headless (no desktop, no Chromium, no kiosk). The countdown on the 4 digit display, the beacon LCD, and the state sent to the backend all come from the UNO's COUNT echo so they never disagree. The equipment unit is only the fan and the e-stop; lockout shows as the fan stopping and STOP WORK on the beacon LCD.

You cannot run hardware tests from here. For every step, tell the human exactly what to flash, what to wire, what to run, and what they should see, using the TEST lines in the guide.

Suggested: pyserial, websockets, gpiozero, pygame or aplay for audio. Pre rendered WAV clips for the fixed voice lines.

Guide: docs/hardware-guide.md. Appendix C is the gateway program map and its local rules (the guide has no Appendix D). Gateway setup, the on-device TEST list, and the laptop simulator are in gateway/README.md. The beacon LCD lines and the IR key meanings are in contracts/serial.md.

## Decisions
- gateway: uv with pyproject.toml (venv from the system python with --system-site-packages so gpiozero finds the Pi 5's lgpio), started by beacon-gateway.service.
- gateway: voice is Piper (en_US-lessac-medium, es_MX-ald-medium, offline) with espeak-ng as the fallback; the Pi 5 has no 3.5 mm jack, so the speaker is USB. Piper's macOS wheel is broken (hard coded espeak data path), so on a laptop it falls back to say or log.
- gateway: the Pi has no microphone, so offline the laptop's BANG cannot arrive. A USB mic on the Pi, if present, pairs flash and bang locally; with none, a flash while offline fails safe to a stop work with distance unknown. Demo step 7 works literally with a mic and as "a flashlight stops the fan" without one.
- gateway: one Roblox bolt reaches the Pi twice (the backend's STOPWORK at once, the local flash to bang ~37 s later at 8 mi); a trigger whose strike time is within 5 s of the running stop work's is the same strike, so there is no "countdown reset" mid demo.
- gateway: the backend sends 1800 s unscaled, so the Pi applies DEMO_COUNTDOWN_SCALE to every countdown.
- gateway: pod readings go up once a second and at once on a presence or tilt change (the backend only acts on changes and every 5 s). Offline, events (local_stopwork, checkin, pin, estop) are queued to queue.jsonl and replayed in order with original timestamps; readings, state, and count are dropped. 5 s websocket pings notice a dropped hotspot in about 10 s.
- gateway: a stop work or e-stop is saved to lock.json and survives a restart as LOCKED OUT with the motor off until the PIN. A PIN release is "SUPERVISOR" on the LCD; a dashboard CLEAR shows the name it carries.
- Gateway BACKEND_URL is the full websocket URL including the site: wss://api.omnirisking-it-all-at-swamphacks.us/ws/gateway/12, with SITE_ID=12 (Seminole) and GATEWAY_TOKEN from Chris by DM. The token is appended as ?token=GATEWAY_TOKEN. These live only in firmware/gateway/.env on the Pi. (Slavik)
- controlbox: nothing blocks (serial read a byte at a time, one digit lit per 2 ms pass, padded LCD writes instead of lcd.clear()). LED: blue at boot until the first command, strobing red during STOPWORK, solid red once the stop work clock runs out until CLEAR, yellow during REST, else the INDEX band. The UNO rewrites MM:SS only while line 1 already ends in MM:SS. The servo detaches 0.7 s after each move so it does not jitter. arduino-cli needs `lib install LiquidCrystal Servo` (the IDE bundles them).
- pod: one file main.py. Core 1 samples the photoresistor at 5 kHz and owns the ADC (water too). The flash threshold is the boot resting mean + 6 sd (at least 1500 counts), two samples in a row, 300 ms refractory, and the mean tracks slow light changes. IR is NEC on a hard IRQ with a REMOTE key map (elegoo21 default, car17), and unknown keys print IRCODE:0x... T and H are left out of the line until the DHT11's first good read (gateway and backend take a missing T/H as None). Flashed from the Pi with mpremote, see pod/README.md.
- build follows Slavik's v5 guide: radar on UART0 GP0/GP1, WS2812B strip on D6, no crew digit; the 4 digit display's f and g wires are swapped on this build and the segment table matches.
- gateway and controlbox, bring up fixes: FLASH lines in the first 2 s after the pod port opens are stale and ignored; the offline fail-safe arms only after 3 s without the backend (so in demo step 7 wait for BEACON OFFLINE plus a few seconds before the flash); a BRIEFING never covers a stop work, lockout, or PIN entry; the speaker is set to 100% before every line; the UNO resyncs and redraws the LCD every 5 s (a motor kick mid write can garble it), resends the strip color every second, and moves the needle 2 degrees per 15 ms.
